For the maintenance team at your hotels, managing requests should be simple and efficient. The BigChange Customer Portal provides a straightforward, user-friendly interface to do just that.

Requesting a New Job

When a new issue arises, the hotel's maintenance team can easily log a new job. The process is quick and intuitive, ensuring no details are missed.

Real-time Updates

Once a job is requested, it's immediately sent to UKFM to arrange accordingly. The status updates automatically as the job progresses, so the maintenance manager can see its status at a glance: Job Confirmed, Engineer on the Way, Job Complete etc.

You can also filer jobs by multiple other parameters, such as date of booking or location if viewing from a 'head office' level, allowing you to easily view each location.

Accessing Past Job History

Need to find a past job report? All completed jobs and their associated documents—like photographs, notes, reports and certification—are stored securely in the portal. Hotel staff can filter by date or status to instantly access the information they need, eliminating the hassle of misplaced paperwork.
